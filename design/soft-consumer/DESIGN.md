# Soft Consumer
Feel: a friendly consumer app. Warm, rounded, touch-first, one thing per screen.
Type: prefer "Figtree" 400/600 (DM Sans ok); body 17/26, headings 28/34 and 22/28, no all-caps.
Color: bg #FFFDF9, surface #FFFFFF, text #24211E, muted #77716A, line #EEE8E0, accent warm coral #E8603C with tint #FDEAE3, success sage #5B8C6A. One accent; no purple or blue-violet gradients.
Layout: single column max 560px mobile-first, 16/24/32 spacing, 44px minimum touch targets, bottom-anchored primary action.
Components: radius 16 on cards and 999 on buttons and chips; soft shadow 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.04), 0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,.06); no colored side borders; simple flat illustrations optional.
Copy: plain and short, second person, no exclamation marks in errors, say what happened and what to do next.
Motion: 240ms ease-out, gentle scale 0.98 on press; respect prefers-reduced-motion.
Never: dense tables, more than one primary button per screen, purple, emoji as icons.
