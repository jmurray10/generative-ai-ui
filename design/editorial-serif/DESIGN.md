# Editorial Serif
Feel: a well-set magazine feature. Calm, confident, lots of air. Reading first.
Type: display "Newsreader" 600 (fallback Georgia); body "Source Serif 4" 400 at 18/30; UI labels "IBM Plex Sans" 500 at 13, letter-spacing 0.02em, small caps for eyebrows. Scale 13/15/18/24/36/56. Max line length 68ch.
Color: paper #FAF7F2, ink #1C1A17, muted #6B645A, rule #E4DED3, one accent oxblood #8A2B2B for links and the single primary action only. No gradients.
Layout: 12-col, 72px outer margin, content in cols 3–10, generous 48/96 vertical rhythm. Images full-bleed or column-aligned, never floating cards.
Components: hairline 1px rules instead of boxes; cards have no shadow and no colored side borders; buttons are text-weight with underline on hover, primary is filled oxblood, radius 2px. Tables: no zebra, hairline row rules, numerals tabular.
Motion: 200ms opacity/translate only. No bounce.
Never: drop shadows on cards, pill badges everywhere, emoji in UI, purple, more than one accent.
