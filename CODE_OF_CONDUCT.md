# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project welcomes everyone. Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion
constructive and on-topic.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or
demeaning comments, and publishing others' private information without consent.

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, and other project communication —
and to public spaces when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **jeff.murray@alumni.upenn.edu**. Reports are reviewed and
handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions and
comments that violate this code, and may ban contributors for serious or repeated
violations.

This is a condensed code of conduct inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
